About the clinic

Small enough to know you. Equipped enough to treat you.

Willow Grove started as a single family-practice office and grew, one referral at a time, into a full clinic — without losing the habit of unhurried visits. Our doctors keep smaller patient loads on purpose, so fifteen-minute appointments don't feel like six.

Today we offer general practice, pediatrics, and a growing set of specialist services, all under one roof at 214 Larkspur Avenue, with a lab and pharmacy pick-up point on site.
